- **Majors:** (1) the Village fence is 4 studs and the range's movement is jump 18 + double jump 12 + dash 20
  with void beyond the floor, so a hop ended the run: now an invisible wall (`wall.barrierHeight` 80,
  `barrierThickness` 8) plus a free rescue back onto a spawn (`player.rescue`). (2) On landscape phones of

  `CanQuery = false`, which the engine ignores on a collidable part: the camera and shots would have hit it.
  **An invisible wall that must not block rays needs its own collision group** (`ArenaBarrier`, colliding only

  `math.clamp` throws when rounding leaves min above max: use `math.min(math.max(x, lo), hi)` for bounds that
  come from layout arithmetic.
