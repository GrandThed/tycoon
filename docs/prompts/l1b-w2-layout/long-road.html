The winding road (recommended)

West to east, the road zigzags through the forest: the gap and the creek, the climb by the lookout, then the three camps, each crossed along its north side so the camp sprawls south of the road. A careless walker on the road is seen by each camp's watch; a careful one hugs the inside of the north fence (the pink line) and passes every resting eye beyond its sight. Each open camp wakes in two or three chunks, not all at once.

The switchback (alternative)

The road folds back on itself up a hillside in three terraces (0, 8, 18, 28): east along the valley through the Pickets, a hairpin up and west to the Main Camp, a second hairpin up and east along the high road, which overlooks the Main Camp, to the Chief at the top. The camps are crossed corner to corner. Past the Main Camp the sneak path is a goat track outside its fence.
