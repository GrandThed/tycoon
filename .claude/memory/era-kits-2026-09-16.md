Decided by Ben on 2026-09-16 during M8, after two Boomtown attempts were built and judged on contact sheets:

- **retro-urban-kit (brick):** rejected. Ben said "I don't really love it."
  - It has 22 tiling textures, which the one-texture merge can't handle.
  - Blueprints are in git history only.
- **city-kit-commercial + city-kit-roads:** Ben liked it, but it overlapped Metropolis.
  - Moved to `tools/testfit/blueprints/_drafts/Metropolis-city-kit-commercial/` as the Metropolis
    starting point.
  - Strips are in `assets/testfit/out/_drafts-Metropolis-city-kit-commercial/`.
- **Final split:**
  - **Boomtown** = `city-kit-suburban` + `city-kit-industrial` (+ roads props).
  - **Metropolis** = `city-kit-commercial` incl. skyscrapers (+ roads).
  - Each kit belongs to one era.
  - The four City Kits share a palette, so the eras differ by building type and height.

Ben wanted the Boomtown redo in a **new session**. The ready prompt is
`docs/prompts/boomtown-suburban-industrial.md`. M9 starts from `docs/prompts/m9-city-dressing.md`.

Ben also wants each era's strips and contact sheet in their own folder: `assets/testfit/out/<Era>/`.

**Why:** a fresh session would otherwise follow the old spec/skill palette, or reuse commercial pieces
that now belong to Metropolis.

**How to apply:** before any Boomtown or Metropolis blueprint work, read `docs/ASSET_RESEARCH.md` §4
"Era kit decision". Never mix a kit into the other era. See [[assets-direction-2026-09-15]].

**Boomtown shipped on these kits (2026-09-16, same day, Ben: "looks fantastic").** At scale 4
the houses are 3–5 studs tall and the industrial buildings ≤ 7.7, so the first height bands
(11–18 studs for shops) forced stacked office towers that read as Metropolis. Ben chose
**"lower, wider"**: ordinary Boomtown slots are ≤ 13 studs and grow by width and clutter; only
Fire Station 18.7 < Radio Station 21.3 < Clock Tower 25.8 are tall. That leaves height to
Metropolis. Reserving signature pieces per slot in the builder brief prevented repeated silhouettes.
Kit facts are in `tools/testfit/README.md` (suburban/industrial section).

**Metropolis shipped 2026-09-18** (commits cf8140d, 4ec25bb, plus the templates commit). Blueprints
had been authored back on 2026-09-16; this session did review → merge → upload → harvest → templates.
89 assets, harvested first try, 24 templates, manifest at 4 eras / 96 models. Heights run
FoodTruck 3.3 → BankTower 21.5 → BroadcastTower 28.6 → SkyscraperA 35.6 (monument), so Metropolis
owns height exactly as the Boomtown "lower, wider" ruling intended.

**A non-monument slot may now exceed 9×9, up to [12, 12]** — Ben's ruling, taken for Metropolis
Stadium after two rejected attempts. `city-kit-commercial` has **no seating or terrace piece**, and
9 studs = 2.25 kit units against 1×1 tiles, so a ring around a pitch always leaves a 1-stud hole
(that hole *was* attempt 1). 12 studs = 3.0 units exactly. Contract wording is in `INTERFACES.md`
under the Blueprints `footprint` key. Two things that made it work:
- **Clearance is computed from harvested extents** (`Scatter.footprintsFor`, RoadGraph), never from
  the 9×9 literal, so a wider slot needs **no dressing-code change** — the "9×9, monument 14×14"
  text in INTERFACES is prose, not a constant.
- The buy pad sits `padOffset` 8 studs off the front (−Z) and is 6×6, so whatever the footprint,
  **keep the front face within ~4.5 studs of the origin** or the pad stops being walkable.

**The testfit camera is in the (+X, −Z) quadrant at ~30°**, so a wall of height h hides ~1.73h
behind it. Attempt 2's bowl hid its own pitch with its *near* side, not its front — if a model's
interior must stay visible, keep **both** +X and −Z low. This, the slant directions and the
ground-surface reads are now in `tools/testfit/README.md`.

**Process note:** three attempts, each one a resumed `SendMessage` to the *same* Opus builder, was
much cheaper than fresh agents — it kept every measured kit fact. It also self-diagnosed honestly
("the pitch is not readable at stage 4") rather than claiming success, so read the strip yourself
but trust the reports' caveats.

**Orbital Colony shipped 2026-09-22** (last content era; M8 complete pending playtest). 24 blueprints,
90/90 uploads first try, one harvest paste (91/0). Kits: `space-kit` + generated **`orbital-kit`**
(`tools/assets/orbital_kit.py`, 21 pieces — domes, solar, flag, hologram, tanks, mast, derrick, lit
strips; Ben approved generating it because space-kit lacks all of those and its only glass piece is
near-black). Rulings: `scale 3.6` + `[12, 12]` for FusionReactor/TerraformStation/SpaceportTerminal
(hex/long hangars are 13/12 studs at 4.0); RoverBay `[12, 9]` (garage fills 8 of 9 studs). Heights:
LaunchTower 42.8 > TerraformStation 23.0 > CommsArray 21.2 > FusionReactor 16.9 ≈ Spaceport 16.5.
- **space-kit's glTF colour factors are sRGB-encoded, not linear.** Decoded per spec its orange
  becomes pale amber (255,208,124) and its dark slate mid-grey; Kenney's own Isometric previews
  confirm raw×255 (255,160,52 / 70,76,87). `palette.py` has `SRGB_FACTOR_KITS = {"space-kit"}`.
  nature-kit is probably the same but Village shipped approved with the decoded look — leave it.
- **space-kit origins are offset ~(+2.0, +1.5)** (baked `tmpParent`), non-uniform; every builder
  needs a place-by-bbox helper. Full kit notes in `tools/testfit/README.md`.
- Judging on a dark-plot render (scratch testfit copy with ground (56,53,60)) caught nothing this
  time because the builders designed for it from the brief — keep the rule in the brief.
