Lessons:
- **Phones never hold fire**: an auto-fire weapon that must hit a world object needs an auto-target for it
  (`WorldReact.Wants`), or touch players can never open the secret.
- **Draw a stream from the shot origin, never the camera** (one `InputController.ShotRay()` for shots and fx).
- **A generated fence is a wall too**: the shot-origin rule (`shooting.originWallChord` 1.5) let forged origins
  through camp C's 1-stud fence; walled camps now set `fence.thickness` 2.5 and check_levels keeps the chord under
  every tall colliding piece. The aim rule's lag lead follows `PositionRing.Motion`'s way, never the client's
  velocity.
- Ability arcs (Leap Smash, Impale Dash) must be checked against every ridge/roof near a ledge; both U-bends can
  still be cut by an arc (~3–4 s, no camp): a known limit until L3 sells a lifting family.
- Next: **W3** ice (frost hammer by lever until the Castle, deep water + rescue, a pond big enough that the
  shrine needs ice), only on Ben's word or after his look.

- **A level element a player can fall into needs a "where did he stand" rule**, not only "where is he now":
  walk-offs and dashes carry the body out of any margin before the kill plane.
- **Anything that turns solid around players needs a body rule and lag in it** (a swing never freezes while
  airborne or around a body; a falling body is led by its fall over the commit lag), and its thickness must stay
  under the root's height so a root is never wholly inside.
- **Coplanar faces shimmer:** the same plane twice (ice vs water, ice vs island) needs an inset or a move; a
  check_levels `ice_coplanar` gate now catches it.
- **Every phone auto-target for a world object needs a cone, an airborne rule and a distance early-out**; scanning
  every cell every frame cost ~0.3 ms on desktop.
- A session limit killed three reviewers mid-run; `SendMessage` resumed each with its reading intact.
