Decided by Ben on 2026-09-17 when combat ("Expeditions") was designed. Contracts:
`docs/INTERFACES.md` "C0 contracts — Expeditions"; plan of record in the session plan file
(`~/.claude/plans/breezy-cooking-fox.md`, copied into `docs/PLAN.md` C0–C3).

- **Second place, same experience.** Combat lives in a separate Roblox place ("Expeditions"),
  built by `combat.project.json`, sharing `src/shared` and the `src/server/Services` folder
  (hub-only services are inert there). Same ProfileStore name/key; hub releases the profile
  before `TeleportAsync`, combat place loads it; never `Steal`.
- **Armory is weapons only, no armor.** Two permanent slots (melee, ranged); **weapons determine
  max HP** (`baseHp + hp(melee) + hp(ranged)`). Tiers cost the era material (Timber/Steel/
  Circuits/Alloy) and **unlock by the city's income per second** (persisted rate). Equipment is
  personal and usable anywhere; nothing is lost on Advance Era or Rebirth.
- **Cash and materials are fixed per enemy** and scale with enemy difficulty (wave growth,
  Overdrive). No Fortify track, no RNG/loot.
- **Co-op:** party from the hub or **join a friend's run in progress** via a MemoryStore run
  registry (reserved servers are not friend-joinable). Rewards split by damage share with a
  floor; Mentor bonus for carrying. Waves scale count only with party size.
- **Rebirth spike:** Ascension forge (all four materials + Valor, cap rises per rebirth) and
  Overdrive difficulty. Tempo director never raises enemy HP/damage; harder is only Overdrive.
- **Combat core:** three-hit melee combo (finisher 1.6×, cleave), ranged = server raycast,
  one ability per weapon on a cooldown that kills shorten (AoE and burst families), pacing
  director merges waves when kill rate is high. Animations: procedural Motor6D first, config IDs
  override later. Enemies: R15 rigs from `CreateHumanoidModelFromDescription` wearing kit meshes.
- **Ben wants every subagent for this work on Opus** (`model: "opus"`), lead on Opus too.
- Milestones are **C0–C3** (M10 stays reserved for icons/thumbnail).

**Why:** a fresh session would otherwise propose hub-side combat, armor slots, Fortify-style
sinks, income-minute cash rewards, or Sonnet/Haiku subagents.

**How to apply:** read the C0 contracts before touching anything under `src/combat`, the
Armory, or `Combat.json`; keep every tunable in `Config/{Armory,Combat,Places}.json` and
`Config/Missions/*.json`. See [[era-kits-2026-09-16]] and [[assets-direction-2026-09-15]].

**Quality verdict 2026-09-23 (after the C2 playtest):** the flow works end to end, but Ben called
the combat itself "pretty outdated and simply bad, like a child's project" — looks, hit feel,
enemy behaviour and controls all. He **parked** it ("we will leave that for later"). A combat feel
pass (code: impact, camera/controls, enemy AI, sounds; then assets: enemy/weapon models, arena
dressing) must come **before C3**; never build more eras on the current feel. Nothing was tuned in
real play yet — every C1 number came from `sim_combat.py`.
